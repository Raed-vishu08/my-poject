Newton School of Engineering's commitment to producing industry-ready engineers aligns perfectly with my teaching philosophy: theory serves the build, and the build reveals the gaps in theory. I would bring a trainer who codes before teaching, builds before explaining, and genuinely loves watching a student's robot come to life for the first time.
